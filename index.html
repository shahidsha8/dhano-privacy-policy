DHANO - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

DHANO is a personal finance app designed to help users record and manage their income and expenses.

2. Account and Internet Access

DHANO does not require users to create an account or register. A PIN may be used to lock access to the app. The app is designed to work offline and does not provide account-based cloud synchronization.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter, such as transaction amounts, dates, categories, account names, and notes, is intended to be stored locally on your device. Transaction storage uses encryption with Android Keystore when encryption is successfully available. This does not mean that every type of app data or exported file is encrypted.

4. Backups and Exports

DHANO may allow you to create backup files and export reports. These files may contain financial information. You choose where to save or share them. If you save files through another app or service, that service may handle the files according to its own privacy policy.

5. Advertising and Data Sharing

DHANO is intended to be provided without advertisements. The app is designed to store information locally, and no developer-operated cloud synchronization is provided. The app's complete release build should be checked to confirm that no advertising or data-transmission components are included.

6. Notifications

If enabled, DHANO may display local reminders or notifications on your device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Backup or exported files saved separately may remain until you delete them yourself.

8. Children's Privacy

DHANO is not specifically designed to target children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.
